License

One of the MagentaDude Audio format's design goals is to be free for anyone to use. That's why the format itself is dedicated to the public domain. You can use it without any restrictions whatsoever.

There's also an official specification and reference implementation available. The specification is licensed CC BY 4.0, and the reference implementation is licensed MIT. However, you can use knowledge from both of those sources without credit of any kind.
